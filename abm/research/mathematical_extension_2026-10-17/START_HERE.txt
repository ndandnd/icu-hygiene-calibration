Research extension prepared 17 October 2026

Purpose
Respond to the request to strengthen the mathematics and assess feasible
robustness/UQ work and specialist-journal readiness. This is a working research
package, separate from the accepted ICU results and current manuscript.

Concrete results
1. A necessary and sufficient linear-algebra criterion for identification of
   a specified coefficient-level policy response from observed bridge features.
2. Sharp response bounds given incomplete bridge information. Under independent
   completion, the baseline survival-weighted mean identifies local response,
   but generally not a finite intervention. Raw and weighted means differ.
3. Under stationary first-order Markov completion and constant independent
   clearing, h,q1,q2 identify efficacy e and persistence rho (interior case).
4. A joint-mechanism interaction: for two opportunities and completion .90 to
   .95, clustering lowers prevention at e=1 but raises it at e=.8. The direction
   changes at e approximately .923528. This is an isolated-bridge result, not
   a newly measured full-ICU effect.

Priority judgment
A reputable specialist-journal paper is a realistic objective. The existing
study has a defensible core. Before submission, prioritize one bounded workflow
robustness study and a useful mathematical/measurement result, instead of a
large undirected grid or more replication of unchanged conditions. A clinical
trial is not a prerequisite for a clearly scoped methodological paper.

The structural design in review_robustness.md is a proposal, not a frozen
protocol or cluster allocation. Its combined alternative tests robustness to
a bundle of rules; it cannot separate each rule's effect. Any future run needs
an explicit question, fixed workload, baseline-only selection, fresh evaluation
and source freeze, while respecting current project-priority constraints.

Uncertainty interpretation
Monte Carlo intervals quantify simulation sampling. Parameter-range and
structural envelopes quantify sensitivity to their declared sets. They become
probabilistic uncertainty statements only with justified distributions or
joint confidence sets. Synthetic inputs here are not clinical observations.
