1. **Local spectrum criterion (Proposition S7).** For a finite nonnegative bridge spectrum shared by both policies and all correlation values, stationary completion without resets gives an explicit derivative of the response at independence. Small-e expansion gives a positive effect; perfect clearing gives a negative effect. The finite-polynomial numerator excludes a zero plateau. Uniqueness, a common root, and a uniform efficacy neighborhood are not claimed.
