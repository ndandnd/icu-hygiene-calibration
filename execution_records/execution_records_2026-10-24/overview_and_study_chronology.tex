\section*{Overview}
This supplement documents the model, the mathematics and every simulation study
behind the main text. All results come from a synthetic agent-based ICU model.
All intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty within that fixed model; they
exclude clinical-input and structural uncertainty and, unless stated,
calibration-selection and interpolation uncertainty. Nothing here is clinical
evidence or a validated description of clinical practice.
Table~\ref{tab:flow} lists the studies in the order they were done, with the
section that reports each. ``Prespecified'' means that the protocol was saved
before that study's outcomes existed; several studies were designed after
earlier results were known, and each section says which comparisons are post
hoc.

The model is synthetic and has no clinical
validation. Carrier screening is ideal and immediate; the .6 isolation
multiplier has no recovered clinical source; lengths of stay are geometric;
bedside sites are binary, with no natural decay; the outcome is colonization,
not infection; a single transmission probability governs every exchange. The
leading-order limit is a fixed-horizon $p\to0$ quantity without a finite-$p$
error bound, and route labels are descriptive, not causal.

with the revision notes. Study 16 is a separately prespecified coefficient
extension (23 October), reported in Section~\ref{sec:s-coef-extension}. A post hoc
worker-reweighting analysis (24 October) and directional-transfer interpretation
appear in Section~\ref{sec:s-directional}.
