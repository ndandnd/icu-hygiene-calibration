\paragraph{Verification and limits.} A separate algorithmic verifier reproduced
every random count from the declared seed streams, enumerated completion words
explicitly and recomputed every defined bound (285,924 projected endpoint
comparisons; maximum discrepancy $2.45\times10^{-15}$); it was written after
results were seen and is not called blind. A separate scientific reviewer
recomputed every summary from the raw archive (834 assertions, no mismatch). The
result requires known $h$, independently initialized assays, stationary
first-order Markov completion, homogeneous clearing and unchanged $\rho$ and $e$
under the policy. Naturally occurring contamination episodes may select
completion states in practice (in the model they do not; Table~\ref{tab:face}),
measurement error in $h$ is excluded, and nothing here estimates full-ICU
finite-$p$, calibration or workflow uncertainty. Whether such assays are
available or practicable clinically is not established; completion sequences
from individual electronic monitoring, with technique or culture audits of $e$,
are an alternative route to the same parameters, and their lag-two
autocorrelation distinguishes the Markov law ($\rho^2$) from the non-Markov
mixture above ($\rho$).
