\paragraph{Profiles.} The three primary profiles are independent misses
$(\rho,e)=(0,1)$, clustered misses $(.5,1)$ and imperfect clearing $(0,.8)$.
They are alternative model assumptions, not estimates of staff behaviour or
washing efficacy. Under independence, one-opportunity survival depends on $h$
and $e$ only through the product $he$, the classical compliance-times-efficacy
reduction of hygiene to a single effective parameter
\citep{austin1999,beggs2009}; the independent-versus-imperfect contrast is of this
kind. Serial dependence at fixed per-boundary mean completion is not: independent and
clustered misses have the same $h$ and the same $q_1$, and differ only from
$q_2$ onward (Table~\ref{tab:qtable}).
