\paragraph{Opportunity intensity.} The care-zone clock generates about 207
boundary events per occupied patient-day, or about 8.6 per patient-hour, about two per
zone visit (Table~\ref{tab:face}). A WHO-style count, which merges each exit with
the next entry \citep{who2009}, would be roughly one per visit, about 104 per
occupied patient-day or 4.3 per patient-hour. We do not compare these with
published opportunity rates, because observational and electronic studies count
different units (WHO observation also adds Moments 2 and 3 and aseptic tasks;
electronic systems may count room entries and exits), and numerical proximity
would not validate the entry/exit rule. The full-ICU limit under the inherited
per-contact clock was not computed; the clock comparisons below change both the
rule and the selected $p$. Table~\ref{tab:roles} gives the model's denominators.
