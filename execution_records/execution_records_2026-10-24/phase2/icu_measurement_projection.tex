\paragraph{ICU projection through the computed coefficient grid (prespecified extension).}
The secondary analysis in the 23 October protocol reused all saved 8,000-per-assay
confidence limits. It replaced the round-1 renewal-plus-residual surrogate with
bilinear interpolation of the 132 directly computed ICU limit values from
Section~\ref{sec:s-coef-extension}, on $\rho\in[0,.95]$, $e\in[.5,1]$.
The feasible-set search remained 81 efficacy slices and 61 correlation points
per nonempty slice. Any set reaching outside the computed domain was to be
counted and excluded, without clipping or extrapolation. The four truths gave
978, 985, 999 and 1,000 retained sets; 22, 15, 1 and 0 were empty, respectively,
and none was out of domain. An independent analytic domain check agreed with
all 4,000 classifications.

Median widths were 5.26, 0.98, 6.66 and 11.62 percentage points
(Table~\ref{tab:extension-projection}), conditional on nonempty retained sets.
As the protocol specified, the superseded round-1 surrogate was evaluated on
exactly these sets for comparison only (6.17, 0.92, 6.66 and 11.67 points).
The protocol called this an ``exact ICU projection'', but that label
is too strong: coefficient values integrate survival on sampled histories,
then the projection interpolates a finite parameter grid and samples the
feasible set. It does not certify continuous ICU extrema or bound interpolation
error, and it does not propagate background uncertainty in the coefficient
surface. A post hoc check against ten separately computed crossing nodes,
not used as interpolation nodes, found discrepancies up to 0.656 percentage
points in $F^*$; this is a diagnostic at those points, not a global error bound
or a bound on interval widths. No new measurements or coverage guarantee are added;
in particular, these widths do not inherit the isolated-bridge continuous
optimization certificate. Empty sets remain reported, and no new $q_1$-only
ICU projection was computed, so these widths cannot show whether adding $q_2$
narrows the ICU forecast; the combined-truth joint width (11.62 points) is close
to the isolated $q_1$-only width (11.4 points).
