\paragraph{Source status and gate counts (post hoc, round 1).} Patient-pickup
acquisitions also record whether the source carrier was imported or acquired
(Table~\ref{tab:source}; 20,000 paired whole-seed bootstrap draws, seed
20261022). Acquisitions from imported carriers form the class that
$p^2\overline K_h$ describes; in all six cells their fall under the policy has a
95\% interval containing the profile's limit. Acquisitions from acquired
carriers come from chains with two gated hand segments (carrier to patient, then
that patient to another); in all six cells their interval contains
$1-(1-F^*)^2$. This is consistent with the hypothesis that the policy ratio of a
path class is approximately the product of the survival ratios of its gated
segments, in which case multi-gate chains would push $F$ above the limit and
ungated segments pull it below. The counts are small for acquired sources (7 policy acquisitions
in the independent profile), and the analysis was defined after the outcomes
were known. Table~\ref{tab:gap} decomposes the original-workflow independent
gap by route label as bookkeeping: with count-based $F$, $F^*-F=\sum_\ell
\mathrm{share}_\ell(F^*-\mathrm{reduction}_\ell)$, and carry-over-labelled
acquisitions account for $+8.72$ of the 7.55 percentage points; this identity
attributes no causal effect to any route or rule.
