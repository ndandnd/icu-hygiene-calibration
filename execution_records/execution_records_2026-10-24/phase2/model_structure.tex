The simulator revisits the ICU agent-based model of an IDWeek 2022 conference
abstract \citep{baek2022}, with clinician-informed schedules recovered from that
project: 30 beds (16 isolation, 14 shared), 30 bedside sites and 19 staff role
slots. Organisms move between patients only through workers' hands and bedside
sites; there is no direct patient-to-patient contact. Patients are admitted and
discharged, some arrive carrying the organism, and acquired carriers can become
later sources. The outcome is newly acquired colonization (carriage), not
infection; the model does not represent progression to infection or death. A
single probability $p$ governs pickup onto hands, transfer to a patient and
exchange between hands and sites. This shared probability is a simplifying
assumption, not a measured biological constant. Pickup and transfer trials at a
contact have probabilities $pc$ and $pd$ with location weights $c,d\in\{1,.6\}$:
the isolation multiplier .6 applies to contacts with patients and sites in
the 16 isolation locations, whatever the patient's carriage status, so it also
protects susceptible patients in single rooms: it is a single-room effect, not
contact precautions applied to carriers. It scales specified contact-transfer
trials; it is not an observed 40\% reduction in acquisition, and we have not
recovered a clinical source for it. Table~\ref{tab:params} lists the settings.
