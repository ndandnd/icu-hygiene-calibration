\paragraph{Surface-disabled conditions at the original probabilities (11--12
October).} The opportunity-rule follow-up also disabled surface exchange at each
profile's fixed original probability on the same 512 holdout seeds
(Table~\ref{tab:rule-conditions}). For independent misses the baseline fell from
2.83 to 0.70 per 1,000 patient-days while relative prevention rose from 67.6\% to
77.3\%; mean acquisitions prevented fell from 3.13 to 0.88 per run. That
contrast was resolved ($+9.7$ [2.9, 16.4] percentage points, 99.167\%), whereas
the clustered ($\mn4.5$ [$\mn13.1$, 3.6]) and imperfect ($+0.6$ [$\mn10.6$,
11.5]) contrasts were not. These matched-seed worlds retain downstream feedback
and are not identical contact histories.

\paragraph{Leading-order versus measured rates.} With the original-workflow
coefficients, the leading-order rate $1000p^2\overline K_h/D^{(0)}$
($D^{(0)}=1{,}640.36$ mean observation patient-days) is close to the measured
environment-disabled rates and far below the full-model rates
(Table~\ref{tab:leading}). With surfaces disabled at the original probabilities,
baseline rates were 0.70, 1.41 and 1.66 against leading-order values of 0.69,
1.44 and 1.59. This is a descriptive comparison of point estimates, not an error
bound or equivalence test.
