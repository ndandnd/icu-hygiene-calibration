\paragraph{Baseline-mismatch estimator.} The change in $F$ associated with adjusting
the control rate $B$ from its observed value to 3.0 was estimated using the slope
$\beta_B=\mathrm dF/\mathrm d\ln B$ (percentage points of $F$ per unit of
natural-log baseline) was estimated from seed-paired original-workflow
conditions that vary $p$ and report both $B$ and $F$
(Table~\ref{tab:slope-inputs}); a 10\% higher baseline changes $F$ by
$\ln(1.1)\beta_B\approx0.095\beta_B$. The implied bias of an observed $F$ is
$F(B_{\mathrm{obs}})-F(3.0)\approx\beta_B\ln(B_{\mathrm{obs}}/3)$; a positive
value means the observed $F$ is higher than it would be at 3.0. Intervals are
percentile 95\% intervals from 20,000 whole-seed bootstrap draws (seed 20261021),
with one random stream per seed bank so that conditions sharing seeds share
draws. The original holdout was resampled in four 128-seed strata so that its
overlap with the independent upper seeds is exact; its recomputed $F$ intervals
differ from the published 200,000-draw intervals by at most 0.12 percentage
points. All point values reproduce the archived reports (52 of 52 checks). An
independent verifier, without reusing the addenda code, reproduced all 149 integer
counts, 510 of 515 point estimates and all 199 interval endpoints within Monte
Carlo tolerance; the five exceptions all came from Monte Carlo half-widths taken
from one-decimal intervals, which are recomputed here from the unrounded archived
intervals (Section~\ref{sec:s-selprop}).
