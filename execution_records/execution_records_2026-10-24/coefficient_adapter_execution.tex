\paragraph{Implementation and gates.} A new adapter composes the frozen bridge
background hooks with the frozen workflow-variant relocation and clearing rules
without editing either file.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Gate 1 (reproduction): passed.} With the alternative rules switched
off, the adapter reproduced archived original-workflow records bitwise on 8
seeds (4 originally produced locally and 4 on the cluster; 4 in bank A and 4 in
bank B): identical ordered bridge-event hashes and relative error 0 in every
$K$. Two of these seeds also matched all 22 surface pairs bitwise. The
independent reviewer reproduced two further seeds with a separately written
script, including every field of the archived record and all 22 pairs.
\item \emph{Gate 2 (rule semantics): passed.} Fourteen unit tests passed (no
swaps; moves only into empty isolation beds in the frozen bed order; deferral
when none is empty; clearing at discharge and relocation; unchanged random-number
state), and an opt-in full-horizon test passed. The reviewer stepped the adapter
and the frozen alternative-workflow model in lockstep for all 378,432 ticks on two seeds:
patient positions, flags, counters and the final random-number state never
diverged, across 19 relocations and 10,845 deferral ticks.
\item \emph{Gate 3 (independent code review): approve with minor notes.} The
notes concerned operational recovery after abnormal termination (a runner that
could hang if a worker were killed, and a possible spurious failure file after a
forced kill), the wording of prediction 1 against rounded published values, the
two possible readings of the prediction-3 comparator, and cosmetic issues. None
affects a coefficient, an event stream or a summary statistic. The review also
verified atomic, resumable record writing, absence of state carry-over when a
worker process is reused, and the bootstrap and delta computations.
\item \emph{Budget rule.} The protocol capped local compute at 80 CPU-hours with
at most 10 concurrent simulation processes, with a fallback (primary profiles on
256 seeds, full surface on bank A only) if the full surface was projected above
the cap. A 3-seed benchmark projected 15.7 CPU-hours, so the full 22-pair surface
ran on all 256 seeds. Actual cost was 14.3 CPU-hours (10 workers, 1~h~46~min
wall); 256 of 256 records completed, with no failure and no seed replacement.
\item \emph{Determinism and recomputation.} The three benchmark seeds were
recomputed in the batch and their scientific fields were identical to the
benchmark records. A separate recomputation of all 22 limits directly from the
raw records of both workflows agreed with the study summary to every printed
digit.
\item \emph{Disclosure.} Before launch, the implementing agent saw the 3-seed
benchmark outcomes while testing the summary script. Nothing in the protocol,
settings or code changed afterwards, and the budget decision was mechanical.
\end{itemize}
